Frequently Asked Questions

What are the college admission requirements? +
Students must submit their academic documents, identity proof, photographs, and completed admission form during the admission process.
How can I check my examination timetable? +
Students can check the examination timetable on the official college website or through the student portal.
How can I apply for leave? +
Students can apply for leave through the student portal or submit a leave application to their department.
Where can I find study materials? +
Study materials can be accessed through the student portal, department website, or obtained from the respective faculty members.
How can I contact my department? +
You can contact your department office during working hours or send an email to the official department email address.
How can I access the college library? +
Students can access the college library using their valid student ID card during the library's working hours.
